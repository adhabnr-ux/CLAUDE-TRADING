## Thesis

The S&P 500 is near all-time highs (~7,350) with stretched valuations (forward P/E 20.9x vs
18.9x ten-year average). Bond yields are rising sharply (30yr briefly >5.19%, 10yr at 4.687%),
the Fed is on hold at 3.5–3.75% with cuts pushed to late 2027, and geopolitical uncertainty
(Iran war, oil supply disruption) is elevated. This environment favors:

1. **Cyclicals with real earnings** over high-P/E growth — Energy, Industrials, and select
   Consumer Defensive are leading the market (+13–22% YTD) while tech has slowed.
2. **Quality cash flow businesses** — rising yields punish speculative premiums; companies
   with strong free cash flow and buybacks are more resilient.
3. **Selective stock-picking** — with SPY itself dragged by lagging mega-cap tech, beating
   it is achievable by concentrating in the sectors that ARE outperforming.

Edge: overweight the three leading macro themes (energy/infrastructure, defense/aerospace,
cost-conscious consumer) while keeping exposure well below SPY's tech-heavy weighting.

## Universe

- US-listed stocks, price ≥ $5 (no penny stocks)
- Primarily large- and mid-cap ($10B+ market cap)
- No options, no shorting, no margin, no crypto, no ETF-only closet indexing
- Sectors of focus: Energy, Industrials (defense/aerospace/construction), Consumer Defensive,
  Healthcare (GLP-1/medical devices), and select Semiconductors where thesis is specific

---

## Entry Signals

All three conditions should be met before entering:

1. **Fundamental catalyst** — strong earnings growth, analyst upgrade, product launch, or
   sector tailwind (e.g. elevated oil price, infrastructure spending, cost-conscious consumer)
2. **Reasonable valuation** — forward P/E not wildly above sector average; prefer stocks with
   FCF yield > 3% or earnings growth that justifies premium
3. **Technical confirmation** — pullback to support or breakout from consolidation, not chasing
   a vertical single-day move

---

## Sizing

- **Starter position:** 7–9% of portfolio (~$7,000–$9,000 initially)
- **Full position:** up to 15% if thesis strengthens after first 4–6 weeks
- **Hard cap:** 20% per CLAUDE.md
- Prefer whole-share quantities to ensure trailing-stop orders are accepted
- Start small and add only when the thesis is confirmed by price/earnings action

---

## Exit Signals

- 10% trailing stop placed immediately after every buy fill (per CLAUDE.md)
- Midday -7% rule: close any position >7% below entry price at the 12:30 PM check
- Thesis breaks: guidance cut, sector reversal, or valuation becomes indefensible
- Discretionary trim: if a position grows to >18% of portfolio on unrealized gains, consider
  trimming back to 15% to rebalance

---

## Cash Policy

- **Minimum cash:** 5% at all times (CLAUDE.md guardrail)
- **Target cash (first 4 weeks):** 40–60% — build gradually, do not rush deployment
- **Steady-state target:** 15–25% cash once the portfolio is fully built (8–12 positions)
- Raise cash if: market shows clear distribution (multiple distribution days), bond yields
  spike further, or macro deteriorates significantly
