## 1. Thesis

The market is rotating from growth/tech into "real economy" sectors. Three structural tailwinds drive the edge:

1. **Energy supply shock**: The Strait of Hormuz has been near-blocked since late February 2026 (≈4 tankers/week vs. 102 average), pushing oil up ~12% YTD. Even with diplomatic noise (Trump/Iran), supply infrastructure takes time to normalize — energy majors with low-cost assets and strong FCF are repricing higher.
2. **Historic defense buildout**: The Trump administration proposed the largest defense budget in US history for FY2027 (~$1.5T). Combined top-5 defense-prime backlogs exceed $700B, providing multi-year revenue visibility that few other sectors can match. Defense ETF (ITA) +38% YTD in 2026.
3. **Consumer defensive rotation**: Consumer confidence is near historic lows. Consumers trade down to value and necessities. Quality staples with pricing power and high brand loyalty benefit while discretionary struggles.

The Fed is on hold at 3.5–3.75%, with an easing bias (new Chair Kevin Warsh takes over June 2026). This "Goldilocks" floor keeps rates from crushing multiples but won't reinflate speculative growth plays quickly. Our edge: early positioning in high-quality cyclical value with identifiable catalysts, before the rotation fully plays out.

**This is NOT closet-indexing.** We are deliberately underweight mega-cap tech vs. SPY, and overweight energy, defense, and staples — where fundamentals justify it.

## 2. Universe

- US large/mid-cap equities, price > $5/share, high daily liquidity (average volume > 1M)
- Focus sectors: Energy (XOM, CVX), Defense/Aerospace (LMT, HWM), Consumer Staples (WMT, PG), Healthcare (UNH), select Tech (MSFT, AMZN)
- ETFs eligible (e.g., ITA for defense, XLE for energy) if individual-stock conviction is low
- **Ineligible:** options, shorts, margin, crypto, penny stocks (<$5), day trading

## 3. Entry Signals (need 3 of 4)

1. **Sector tailwind**: name sits in a favored sector with structural catalyst
2. **Earnings momentum**: beat + raised guidance in most recent quarter
3. **Valuation not stretched**: fwd PE <30× for defense/industrials, <25× for energy/staples, <35× for quality tech
4. **Entry timing**: price on a pullback or consolidation (not extended >10% above 50-day MA)

## 4. Sizing

| Conviction | Notional | % of $100K |
|-----------|----------|------------|
| Starter   | $8K–$10K | 8–10%      |
| Standard  | $10K–$15K| 10–15%     |
| High      | up to $20K| up to 20% |

- Use **whole-share quantities** (not notional) so trailing-stop orders are always possible.
- Calculate shares = floor(target_notional / current_price).
- Never exceed 20% of total portfolio value in any single position.
- Typical starter is well below the 20% cap — conviction earns size.

---

## 5. Exit Signals

- **10% trailing stop** — placed immediately after every new long fills (mandatory)
- **−7% midday rule** — close any position down >7% from entry by 12:30 PM ET
- **Thesis broken** — earnings miss + guidance cut, regulatory action against core business, sector reversal (two consecutive weeks of sector underperformance vs. SPY)
- **Valuation stretched** — position has run >40% and now trades at material premium to fair value with no new catalyst; trim or exit

---

## 6. Cash Policy

- **Hard floor:** ≥5% cash at all times
- **Target during build phase (weeks 1–2):** 70–80% cash; deploy gradually
- **Steady-state target:** 15–25% cash (dry powder for opportunities and risk management)
- Hold more cash when macro uncertainty is elevated (current environment qualifies)

## 8. Operating principles

- A day with no trades is correct when conviction is low.
- Never trade to look active. Every new position needs a written thesis.
- Sell on thesis breaks, trailing stops, or the −7% rule — not on noise.
- Cross-check any trade-driving news against a second source before acting.
- Keep lessons.md updated; re-read it every run.
