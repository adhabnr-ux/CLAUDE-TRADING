## 1. Thesis

The macro backdrop is a resilient economy with re-accelerating inflation, causing Treasury yields to surge (10Y ~4.69%, 30Y ~5.20%, 18-year high). The Fed is on hold and may hike before year-end. This punishes long-duration / high-multiple growth stocks and rate-sensitive sectors (REITs, utilities).

However, a powerful secular tailwind is running underneath: the AI infrastructure buildout is generating real revenue and earnings, not just hype. Semiconductor and hyperscaler revenue is growing 28–85% YoY. Energy is getting a geopolitical premium (Iran tensions) on top of supply discipline. Quality companies with pricing power and durable earnings can outperform SPY in this environment.

**Edge vs SPY:** Selective concentration in the sectors and names where earnings momentum is strongest AND valuation is not yet stretched. Avoid closet-indexing and rate-sensitive names.

---

## 2. Universe

- US large/mid-cap stocks, price ≥ $5, liquid (avg volume > 500K shares/day)
- Eligible sectors: Technology (AI/semis/cloud), Energy (oil & gas), Defense & Aerospace, Healthcare (quality growth), Financials (rate beneficiaries)
- Avoid: penny stocks, options, shorting, margin, crypto, REITs, utilities, pure-growth with no earnings

---

## 3. Entry Signals

Open a position only when ALL of the following apply:

1. **Earnings momentum:** Company beat last quarter's EPS/revenue estimates AND raised forward guidance
2. **Sector tailwind:** In a favored sector (AI infrastructure, energy, defense, select healthcare, financials)
3. **Valuation reasonable:** Forward P/E < 30x for tech/growth; < 20x for value/cyclicals. PEG < 2.5
4. **Not parabolic:** Stock is not up >25% in the prior 30 days (avoid chasing breakouts)
5. **Written thesis:** Can state why this name, why now, and what would disprove it

Optional confirmation: Pulling back to a 20- or 50-day moving average is a favorable entry; do not require it.

## 4. Sizing

| Conviction | Position size (% of portfolio) | Dollar amount ($100K) |
|------------|-------------------------------|----------------------|
| Starter / exploratory | 5–7% | $5,000–$7,000 |
| Normal / supported thesis | 8–12% | $8,000–$12,000 |
| High conviction | 13–17% | $13,000–$17,000 |
| Maximum ever | 20% | $20,000 |

- Target 8–12 positions total
- No single position enters at > 12% (high-conviction add-ons can bring to 17–20% over time)
- Enforce CLAUDE.md caps: max 20% single name, max 25% of portfolio in new buys per day, max 3 new positions per week

---

## 5. Exit Signals

In priority order:
1. **10% trailing stop** triggers (auto, placed immediately after every buy)
2. **−7% from entry** at midday check (hard cut, per guardrails)
3. **Thesis break:** earnings miss + guidance cut; fundamental deterioration; loss of competitive position
4. **Parabolic extension:** position > 25% above entry AND valuation has become stretched — trim to starter size
5. **Better opportunity:** if a clearly superior candidate appears and portfolio is at max positions, exit weakest holding

Never sell on day-to-day noise. Never sell just because the market is down.

---

## 6. Cash Policy

- Hard minimum: 5% cash at all times (guardrail)
- Normal target: 10–15% cash
- Elevated caution target (high yield pressure, elevated VIX): 20–30% cash
- Current guidance: Build portfolio gradually over 4–6 weeks. Holding 40–60% cash is acceptable in the first month. Do not rush to deploy.
